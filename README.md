# Egyptian Trade & Export Opportunities

## Project Idea

This project analyzes Egypt's foreign trade data from January 2021 to
December 2025. It examines imports from the world and exports to
international markets by product (HS 6-digit code), trading partner,
quantity, trade value, and relevant economic indicators.

The project aims to assess Egypt's foreign trade performance, understand
import dependencies, evaluate export performance, identify concentrated
supplier and destination markets, and highlight potential product-level
trade opportunities for further investigation.

## KPIs

The project will examine the following indicators, subject to data
availability and validation:

### Overall Trade Performance

-   Total Imports
-   Total Exports
-   Trade Balance
-   Export Coverage of Imports
-   Year-over-Year Import Growth
-   Year-over-Year Export Growth

### Product-Level Analysis

-   Top Imported Products
-   Top Exported Products
-   Product-Level Trade Balance
-   Import and Export Shares by HS Code
-   Product Trade Value and Quantity Trends
-   Unit Value Indicators

### Country & Regional Analysis

-   Top Supplier Countries
-   Top Export Destination Markets
-   Trade Value by Region
-   Import Supplier Concentration (HHI)
-   Export Market Concentration (HHI)

### Opportunity Screening

-   Products with High Import Values
-   Products with Sustained Import Growth
-   Products with Limited or Growing Egyptian Exports
-   Products with Concentrated Supplier Markets
-   Potential Export Market Opportunities

Opportunity indicators are intended for preliminary screening only.
Trade data by itself does not establish domestic production capacity,
commercial feasibility, or investment profitability.
